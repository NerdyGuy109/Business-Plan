Recent sales

Suggested orders refill each ingredient to its par level once it reaches its reorder point. Adjust quantities, then create a purchase order per supplier. Mark it received when it arrives to add the stock.

Purchase orders

When a delivery arrives: pick its order, check each item against what's in the box, note any damage or shortage, then submit. Good items are added to stock; anything missing stays on order.

Receiving log

Drag a shift to move it to another person or day; hold Ctrl (or Alt/Option) while dropping to copy it. Click an empty spot to add a shift, a shift to edit it, or a name to edit that employee.

Download workbook

The workbook is regenerated automatically after every change and saved to exports/inventory.xlsx. It contains: Ingredients, Menu Items, Recipes (which parts go into which item), a Where Used matrix, Procurement, Purchase Orders and the Sales Log. Costs, margins and reorder flags are live formulas.

Import changes from Excel

Edited ingredients, menu items or recipes in Excel? Import the workbook to update the website. Only the blue input columns of Ingredients, Menu Items and Recipes are read; sales and purchase orders are kept.
