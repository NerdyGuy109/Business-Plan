Recent sales

Suggested orders refill each ingredient to its par level once it reaches its reorder point. Adjust quantities, then create a purchase order per supplier. Mark it received when it arrives to add the stock.

Purchase orders

When a delivery arrives: pick its order, check each item against what's in the box, note any damage or shortage, then submit. Good items are added to stock; anything missing stays on order.

Receiving log

Stock count

Count what's really on the shelf and enter it in each item's recipe unit. Leave a row blank to skip it. On-hand stock is set to your count, and the difference is your variance: stock that left without a sale or a waste entry (over-portioning, unlogged waste, mistakes or theft).

Log waste

Anything thrown away, spilled, remade or given away. It comes out of stock and shows in the waste report.

Waste this week

Variance report

Each count compared with what the system expected. A negative variance means stock went missing. "% of usage" compares it with what sales and waste say was used since the last count.

My account

Users

Staff record sales, receive deliveries, count stock, log waste and see the menu and schedule. Managers also run the menu, ingredients, ordering, schedule, imports and Excel. Owners also manage users, settings and backups. Linking a user to an employee fills in their name on receipts, counts and waste.

Backups

A copy of all data is saved automatically every day (the last 30 days are kept), plus an extra copy before every Excel or sales import. Download a full backup (data, users and pictures) regularly and keep it somewhere other than this computer. To restore one, stop the app and run python -m app.backup restore <file>.

Download workbook

The workbook is regenerated automatically after every change and saved to exports/inventory.xlsx. It contains: Ingredients, Menu Items, Recipes (which parts go into which item), a Where Used matrix, Procurement, Purchase Orders and the Sales Log. Costs, margins and reorder flags are live formulas.
