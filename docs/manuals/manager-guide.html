M Menu & Inventory

Manager guide

How to look after your team's accounts, fix common mistakes, and run the daily and weekly routines: sales imports, reordering, deliveries, counts, waste, costs and the schedule. Read the Staff guide first; everything in it applies to you too.

1What you can do

RoleCan
StaffRecord sales, receive deliveries, count stock, log waste, view the menu (with allergens) and the schedule, change their own password.
Manager (you)Everything staff can, plus: the Dashboard, menu and recipes, ingredients and suppliers, ordering, the schedule and employees, sales imports, Excel, removing wrong waste entries, and staff accounts (add, reset passwords, disable).
OwnerEverything, plus manager and owner accounts, roles, business settings (name, target food cost) and backups.

2Helping your team with accounts

All account work happens on Settings → Users. You see your staff (and yourself); managers and owners are handled by the owner.

Add a new team member

  1. Under Add a user, type their name and a username (e.g. riley: letters, numbers, dots or dashes).
  2. Type a temporary password (8+ characters).
  3. Pick their Employee record so deliveries, counts and waste they log carry their name. (Add them on the Schedule tab first if they aren't there; see section 7.)
  4. Tap Add user. Tell them their username and temporary password in person, and give them the Staff guide.

Someone forgot their password

  1. Find them in the Users table and tap Reset password.
  2. Type a new temporary password (8+ characters) and confirm.
  3. Tell them the temporary password. They are signed out everywhere, and at their next sign-in the app makes them choose their own.
"Too many attempts"? After 5 wrong passwords that username is locked for 5 minutes from that device. A reset doesn't lift the wait.

Someone leaves

  1. Tap Disable on their row. They are signed out at once and can't sign in. (Enable brings them back.)
  2. On the Schedule, click their name and tap Remove from roster. Their upcoming shifts become open shifts; past shifts stay for labor history.
Good habits One account per person, never shared. Never ask for someone's password; reset it instead. Disable accounts on someone's last day. You can't reset your own password here: use My account, or ask the owner if you've forgotten it.

3Questions staff will ask you

They sayWhat to do
"I forgot my password" / "It says too many attempts"Reset their password (section 2). If locked, they wait 5 minutes, then sign in with the temporary one.
"It says this needs a manager account"That action is manager-only (e.g. editing the menu or creating orders). Do it for them, or explain the right way.
"Should I ring sales up here?"Only if your shop doesn't import sales from the till. If you import (section 4), tell staff not to also record sales, or every drink is counted twice.
"I logged the wrong waste"Counts & Waste → Waste log, tap Remove on that entry. The stock goes back. Then they log it correctly.
"I entered a sale or a delivery wrong"Sales recorded by hand and delivery receipts can't be undone. Fix the stock: do a stock count of the affected items (section 6). Add a note.
"The delivery has items we didn't order"Have them tick Wrong or extra items and describe it in the notes; call the supplier.
"Is this drink nut-free?"Check the Contains line on the Menu. The app doesn't track shared blenders or cross-contact, so never promise "allergen-free".
"Can I swap shifts?"Only managers change the schedule. Drag the shift to the new person (section 7).

4Every day

a. Open with the Dashboard

  • Need reorder and the Needs reordering table: a red < lead time tag means stock runs out before a new delivery could arrive. Order today.
  • Deliveries due: make sure someone checks them in on Receiving; chase anything overdue.
  • Menu availability: how many of each drink stock can make, and which ingredient limits it.

b. Import yesterday's sales from the till

  1. In your till system, export the item sales report for yesterday as a CSV file (one row per item sold, with date and quantity).
  2. Open Record Sales → Import sales from your till system and choose the file.
  3. Check the column choices (Date, Item name and Quantity are required). They are usually right.
  4. For each name with a red bar, pick the matching menu item, or Ignore for things that aren't drinks (gift cards, merch). The app remembers your choices.
  5. Tap Import … drinks. If a file was imported before, you'll be warned before counting it twice.
Imported the wrong file? Under Import history, tap Undo: its sales are removed and the ingredients go back into stock.

c. Reorder

  1. Open Procurement. Items at their reorder point are grouped by supplier with the number of packages needed to refill to par.
  2. Adjust Packages if needed (e.g. a supplier minimum, or a slow week).
  3. Tap Create purchase order for each supplier.
  4. Place the order with the supplier as you normally do (phone, email, portal). The app records the order; it doesn't send it.

d. Keep an eye on deliveries

Staff check deliveries in on Receiving (Staff guide, section 7). Review the Receiving log for damage, shortages and late deliveries, and follow up with suppliers. If an invoice shows a new price, enter it in the Invoice $/pkg column when receiving: the ingredient's cost updates and the change appears under Cost changes on the Dashboard.

5Every week: schedule

  • Start next week: go to next week (▶) and tap Copy last week, then adjust.
  • Move a shift: drag it to another person or day. Hold Ctrl (or Alt/Option) while dropping to copy it.
  • Add a shift: click an empty spot; use the Open / Mid / Close buttons for common times.
  • Edit or delete: click the shift. On a phone or tablet, change the person or date here (dragging needs a mouse).
  • Fix the warnings: red outline = double-booked; red hours = over their maximum; Open shifts row = nobody assigned yet.
  • Watch labor: the bottom rows show labor cost and labor % of sales for each day.
Employees (people on the schedule) are not the same as users (sign-in accounts). Add someone with + Employee (name, role, hourly rate, maximum hours per week), then give them a user account on Settings if they need to sign in.

6Every week: counts, waste and costs

Stock count and variance

Have staff count the high-value items weekly (fruit, protein, nut butters, cups), at the same time each week, e.g. Sunday close. Then open Counts & Waste → Variance report.

If you seeLikely cause and what to do
Small shortfalls (under ~3%) across many itemsNormal spillage and rounding. Keep counting; watch the trend.
One item consistently shortOver-portioning (check scoops and recipes with staff), unlogged waste, or theft. Remind staff to log waste.
An item over (positive)A delivery received twice, sales counted twice (rung up and imported), or a counting error. Check the receiving log and imports.
Huge differencesWrong unit: counted packages instead of oz/each. Recount; check the "1 pkg = …" line.

Waste

The Waste this week card totals waste by reason. Lots of expired / spoiled: order smaller amounts more often or lower the par level. Lots of remade drink: retrain on the recipes involved. Check staff and free drinks against your policy.

Ingredient cost changes

Every ingredient price change (typed in, from an invoice, or from Excel) shows here with its effect on each drink. When a drink goes above the target, consider a recipe tweak, a price rise or another supplier. The owner sets the target on Settings.

7Menu, ingredients and Excel

Add or change a drink

  1. Menu → + New menu item, or click a drink to edit it.
  2. Add a picture, name, price and description.
  3. Click ingredients to add them (or type and press Enter); set amounts with − / +.
  4. Check the box on the left: cost, margin, food-cost %, a price for your target, and the allergens.
  5. Save item. Duplicate as new is the fastest way to make a size or flavour variation.

Add or change an ingredient

Ingredients → click a row (or + New ingredient). Keep these right, because every cost and reorder depends on them:

  • Quantity in package in the recipe unit, and Usable yield % for produce you trim or peel.
  • Package cost and shipping, lead time, minimum order.
  • Reorder point and par level (use the suggestion from recent usage).
  • Allergens: tick every one the ingredient contains. Drinks pick them up automatically.

Excel

Download inventory.xlsx any time: it is rebuilt after every change with live formulas. You can edit ingredients, menu items and recipes in Excel and import the file back. The import replaces ingredients, menu and recipes with what's in the file (a backup is saved first), so always import the latest download, not an old copy.

8Training new staff

  • Create their account and link it to their employee record (section 2).
  • Give them the Staff guide; walk through signing in and choosing a password together.
  • On their first shift, show them: Record Sales (or tell them sales come from the till), allergens on Menu, logging waste, signing out.
  • Before they receive a delivery alone, do one together.
  • Before they count alone, show how to count in the unit shown ("1 pkg = …") and that blank means skipped.
  • After a week, give them the Staff knowledge check (pass mark 10 out of 12) and go over any wrong answers.

Manager checklist

Every day

  • Dashboard: reorder, out of stock, deliveries due
  • Import yesterday's sales from the till
  • Create purchase orders, then place them with suppliers
  • Check the receiving log for damage, shortages, delays

Every week

  • Copy and adjust next week's schedule; clear the warnings
  • Weekly count of high-value items; review the variance report
  • Review waste by reason
  • Review cost changes and drinks over target

Accounts

  • New starter: add user, link employee, temporary password in person
  • Forgotten password: Reset password → new temporary one
  • Leaver: Disable user, Remove from roster
  • Managers / owners / roles / backups: ask the owner

Fixing mistakes

  • Wrong waste: Waste log → Remove
  • Wrong import: Import history → Undo
  • Wrong sale, delivery or count: recount the affected items
  • Sales counted twice? Don't ring up and import
