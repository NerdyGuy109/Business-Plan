M Menu & Inventory

Staff guide

Everything you need for a shift: signing in, recording sales, checking allergens, receiving deliveries, counting stock, logging waste and checking your schedule. Keep it by the counter.

Before you start Your manager gives you a username and a temporary password, and tells you the app's address (on the shop tablet it is usually already bookmarked). If anything in this guide looks different on your screen, ask your manager.

1Signing in

  1. Open the app on the shop tablet or computer. If you are not signed in you will see the Sign in box.
  2. Type your username and password. Usernames are not case-sensitive; passwords are.
  3. Tap Sign in. You start on the Record Sales tab.
"Wrong username or password" Check caps lock and try again. After 5 wrong tries the app makes you wait 5 minutes before trying that username again. If you still can't get in, see Forgot your password? below.

2Your first sign-in: choose your own password

The first time you sign in with a temporary password, the app asks you to choose your own before you can do anything else.

  1. Type a new password: at least 8 characters, and different from the temporary one.
  2. Type it again in New password again.
  3. Tap Save password and continue.
Keep it to yourself Everything you do is recorded under your name: sales, deliveries you receive, counts and waste. Never share your password or sign in for someone else.

3Forgot your password?

  1. Ask a manager (or the owner). Nobody can look up your old password; they set a new temporary one for you.
  2. Sign in with the temporary password they give you.
  3. The app asks you to choose your own password, exactly as on your first sign-in (section 2).
Locked out after too many tries? A new password doesn't lift the 5-minute wait. Wait 5 minutes, then sign in with the new one.

5Recording a sale

Ask your manager first Many shops bring sales in automatically from the till system each day. If yours does, don't also record sales here, or every drink is counted twice and stock goes wrong.
  1. Open Record Sales. Tap a drink to add it to the Ticket; tap again for another. A green number shows how many are on the ticket.
  2. Fix mistakes on the ticket with − and +, or tap Clear to start over.
  3. Optional: open Ingredients this will use to see exactly what comes out of stock.
  4. Tap Record sale. The ingredients are deducted straight away.

6When a customer asks about allergies

Every drink that contains one of the nine major allergens shows a ⚠ Contains: line on the Menu tab and under its button on Record Sales. The list comes from the drink's ingredients.

  1. Open Menu and find the drink (use the search box).
  2. Read the Contains line, and the ingredient list above it.
  3. Tell the customer what it contains. If they have a serious allergy, follow your shop's allergy procedure.
The app can't see the blender It lists what is in the recipe. It does not know about shared blenders, scoops or cross-contact. Never promise a drink is "allergen-free".

7Receiving a delivery

When a delivery arrives, check what's actually in the boxes against the order before you put it away.

  1. Open Receiving. Expected orders are listed on the left with a badge: due today, due in 2 days, or 3 days late.
  2. Tap the order that matches the supplier's packing slip or invoice (search by order number, e.g. PO-003, or supplier name).
  1. Choose your name in Received by. The date and time fill in by themselves. Type the tracking or invoice number if there is one.
  2. For each item, count the packages:
    • Everything there and in good shape: tick All OK. (If the whole delivery is fine, tap ✓ Everything arrived in good condition.)
    • Something missing or damaged: type how many arrived OK and how many are Damaged, and add a short Note (e.g. "carton leaking").
  3. Tick Outer packaging damaged or Wrong or extra items if that applies, and add anything else in Delivery notes.
  4. Read the green summary: what will be added to stock, and what is still on order.
  5. Tap Submit receipt and confirm.
SituationWhat to do

The delivery is late

Open the order, tap Delivery delayed? Log it, write what happened (e.g. "supplier called, truck a day late"), set the new expected date, and tap Log delay.

8Counting stock

Your manager will tell you what to count and when (often a few high-value items at closing).

  1. Open Counts & Waste. Check your name in Counted by; add a note such as "Sunday close count".
  2. Use the category list or the filter box to find the items you are counting.
  3. Count what is really on the shelf and type it in the item's unit (oz, fl oz, each…). The grey line shows how much is in one package, e.g. 1 pkg = 320 oz: 3 full bags plus a half bag = 960 + 160 = 1,120 oz.
  4. Leave items you didn't count blank (not 0). Blank means "skipped"; 0 means "none left".
  5. Tap Save count and confirm.
Count what you see Don't adjust your count to match "System says". The difference is exactly what the manager needs to know about.

9Logging waste

Log anything that leaves stock without being sold, as soon as it happens.

  1. On Counts & Waste, choose Ingredient (e.g. a bag of spinach) or Whole drink (e.g. a remade smoothie).
  2. Pick what it was and how much (in the unit shown, or number of drinks).
  3. Pick the Reason, check Logged by, add a note if useful.
  4. Tap Log waste.

10Checking your schedule

Open Schedule. Each person has a row and each day a column. Use ◀ and ▶ to change week and This week to come back. Shifts in the Open shifts row still need someone: tell your manager if you can take one. Only managers can change the schedule.

11Changing your password and signing out

Change your password

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under My account, type your current password and a new one (8+ characters).
  3. Tap Change password. Any other device you were signed in on is signed out.

Sign out

Tap Sign out at the top right. Always sign out of a shared tablet at the end of your shift, so nobody else works under your name.

Quick reference

Every shift

  • Sign in with your own account
  • Check allergens on Menu before answering allergy questions
  • Log waste as it happens (Counts & Waste)
  • Sign out at the end of your shift

When a delivery arrives

  • Receiving → pick the matching order
  • Choose your name; add the invoice number
  • Tick All OK, or enter OK / Damaged with a note
  • Check the summary → Submit receipt

When counting

  • Count in the unit shown (1 pkg = …)
  • Blank = skipped, 0 = none left
  • Count what you see, don't match the system

Passwords

  • 8+ characters, only you know it
  • Forgot it? A manager sets a temporary one
  • Change it: Settings → My account
  • 5 wrong tries = wait 5 minutes
