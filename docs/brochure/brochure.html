How it works

01

Build your menu

Click ingredients into each recipe. Cost, margin, food-cost % and allergens appear instantly.

02

Bring in sales

Import each day's sales from your till, or ring them up here. Every drink deducts its ingredients.

03

Reorder & receive

Low items land on a supplier-ready order list, and deliveries are checked in item by item.

04

Count & schedule

Weekly counts show what went missing; drag shifts into place and watch labor cost.

1Live dashboard

  • Reorder alerts, with days of stock left
  • Deliveries due, waste and labor this week
  • Ingredient price changes and drinks over your food-cost target

2Menu builder & allergens

  • Photo, description and recipe in minutes
  • Live cost, margin and a price for your target
  • Allergens rolled up from every ingredient

3Ingredients & suppliers

  • Pack size, cost, shipping, lead time, shelf life
  • Usable yield for trimmed produce: true cost per oz
  • Which drinks use each ingredient

4Till sales import

  • Load the daily export from Square, Toast or Clover
  • Item names matched once, then remembered
  • Refunds skipped; duplicate files blocked; undo

5Ordering

  • Reorder list grouped by supplier
  • Packages to refill to par, with order totals
  • Purchase orders with expected delivery dates

6Receiving deliveries

  • Check each item off against the order
  • Log damage, shortages, late or wrong deliveries
  • Good items go into stock; missing ones stay on order

7Counts & waste

  • Shelf counts against what sales say is left
  • Variance in units, dollars and % of usage
  • Waste logged by reason, with its cost

8Staff scheduler

  • Drag-and-drop weekly schedule; copy last week
  • Flags overtime, double-booking and open shifts
  • Labor cost and labor % of sales by day

9Sign-in, roles & backups

  • Everyone has their own login
  • Staff, manager and owner access; managers reset staff passwords
  • Automatic daily backups, plus a full download

10Live Excel workbook

  • Updated after every change, with live formulas
  • Where-used matrix, counts, waste, weekly hours
  • Edit in Excel and import changes back
